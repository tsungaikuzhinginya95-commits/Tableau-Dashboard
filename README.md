# Tableau-Dashboard
Graduation Rate Analysis 

The Graduation Rate Analysis dashboard examines student graduation outcomes based on 
academic and socioeconomic factors. The overall on-time graduation rate is 43.9%, with 
students taking an average of 4.98 years to graduate. The average college GPA is 3.377, while 
average parental income is approximately $67,378. 

The analysis shows a clear relationship between parental income and on-time graduation. 
Students from higher-income households have higher graduation rates, with the over $100,000 
income group achieving about 63%, compared with the lowest-income group having the 
weakest performance. 

Parental education shows relatively small differences in the average years’ students take to 
graduate. This suggests that parental income may be a more influential factor than parental 
education in determining timely graduation. 

Overall, the findings highlight the importance of financial support, early academic 
intervention, mentoring, and effective student advising to improve graduation outcomes. 
The dashboard also provides an interactive way to identify groups that may need additional 
support.
